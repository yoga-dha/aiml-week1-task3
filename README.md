# AIML Internship Week 1 - Task 3

## Project Title

Business Insight Report on Student Performance Dataset

## Objective

The objective of this project is to transform analytical findings into business-oriented insights and recommendations that can help improve student academic performance.

## Dataset Summary

The dataset contains student demographic, academic, and behavioral information. GPA was used as the primary indicator of academic performance.

## Analysis Approach

* Dataset inspection and understanding
* Data cleaning and preprocessing
* Descriptive statistics
* Data visualization
* Correlation analysis
* Business insight generation

## Key Findings

1. Students who spend more time studying generally achieve higher GPA values.
2. Higher absenteeism appears to be associated with lower academic performance.
3. Most students fall within the average GPA range.
4. Academic performance is influenced by multiple factors.
5. Tutoring and parental support may positively contribute to student success.
6. Students with similar study hours may still achieve different GPA values, indicating additional influencing factors.

## Recommendations

* Encourage consistent study schedules.
* Provide academic support for students with frequent absences.
* Expand tutoring opportunities.
* Increase parental engagement in learning activities.
* Monitor academic performance regularly to identify at-risk students.

## Limitations

* The dataset represents a limited sample of students.
* Correlation does not imply causation.
* Some influencing factors may not be present in the dataset.
* Results may vary across different educational environments.

The analysis indicates that study habits, attendance, and support-related factors influence student performance. Data-driven interventions can help improve academic outcomes.
